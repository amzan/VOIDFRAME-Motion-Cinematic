# Character system and motion vocabulary
The starter includes a small hierarchical cutout rig, deterministic pose math, named poses and pose interpolation. It is a technical scaffold with primitive artwork, not final-quality character assets. Replace primitive silhouettes with authored paths and layered artwork for each episode. Establish neutral pose and consistent local axes: degrees, +x right, +y down, origin at pelvis. Parent torso to pelvis, head to torso, upper arm to shoulder, forearm to elbow, thigh to hip, shin to knee, foot to ankle; use local pivot transforms. Separate near/far layers and swap occlusion deliberately for turns. Never attach face features.

Author poses by beat: anticipation, key action, contact, overshoot and recovery. Test a silent pose silhouette at small size. Use the bundled pose names as references, not a universal mocap library. For a walk/run, key contact/down/passing/up, alternate sides, constrain world foot position during stance, adjust pelvis for weight transfer; no sliding. For reach, solve target in parent coordinates with a two-link IK solution or manually key contact; clamp reach and choose correct bend direction. For props, align hand and object at contact, then change parent or use matched world transform. For bends, keep joints from separating; use overlapping artwork or mesh/path deformation. For dramatic rotation use alternate view assets and silhouette keys; do not scale flat art to zero and call it 3D. Use occasional replacement drawings for smear frames, extreme foreshortening, complex hand actions or impacts. Secondary motion trails primary action: scarf, coat, hair, dust. Keep motion local, purposeful and deterministic.

Minimum reusable movement vocabulary to implement per production: idle/breath, walk, run, stop, look/turn, reach/grab/release, sit/rise, jump/land, stumble/fall, fear recoil, grief slump, determination, and action poses. Implement only needed animations; do not claim unimplemented clips exist. Record key poses, timing, rig ID and orientation in character bible. Assess body acting and no-eye/no-mouth rule in every shot and reflection.
