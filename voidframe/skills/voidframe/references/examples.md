## Motion proof suggestion: The Last Lantern
Original test scene, not a reference recreation. 8 s, 24 fps, 192 frames. A traveler notices a lantern, hesitates, then opens a door to warm light. No dialogue, no required music. Character: charcoal coat, warm rust scarf, rounded smooth blank head. Environment: blue-grey alley, foreground rain, amber lantern, layered distant silhouettes of architecture. Shots: 0–48 establish; 48–108 medium gesture and hesitation; 108–192 opening door and warm light reveal. Each shot needs actual character movement with articulated limbs and a motivated camera. Use subtle rain and cloth follow-through; avoid fake motion by moving whole character as a single sticker. Make vertical and horizontal compositions only if requested.
